Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Zambian Marketplace respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you when using our marketplace.

1. Information we collect

We may collect account information such as your name, email address and profile details; marketplace information such as products, seller details, carts, orders, reviews and favorites; and messages or support requests you send through the platform.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, provide marketplace services, process orders, maintain carts, connect buyers and sellers, provide notifications, prevent abuse and improve the platform.

3. Authentication and account security

Account authentication is handled through our authentication infrastructure. You are responsible for keeping your credentials secure and should contact us if you believe your account has been compromised.

4. Payments

Where payments are offered, payment processing may involve third-party payment providers. Zambian Marketplace does not need to store sensitive payment credentials when they are handled by those providers.

5. Data sharing

We may share information only as needed to provide marketplace services, operate infrastructure, process transactions, comply with applicable law, or protect users and the platform. We do not sell personal information as a marketplace business model.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. You may request account or personal-data deletion, subject to information that must legally be retained.

7. Your choices

You may review or update available account information and request assistance with your personal data through our support channels.

8. Children

The marketplace is not intended for children who are not legally permitted to use online marketplace services. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so we can investigate.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the marketplace evolves. The latest version will be published on this page with its effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, please use the marketplace contact/support page.
